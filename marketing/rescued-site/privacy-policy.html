Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 10, 2026

GatherSync ("we", "us", or "our") provides scheduling and coordination tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use our website and services.

  • Account details such as name, email address, and profile information.
  • Event and scheduling data you create or submit.
  • Communications sent through contact forms or support channels.
  • Basic usage and device data for reliability, analytics, and security.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve GatherSync features.
  • To operate accounts, scheduling workflows, and notifications.
  • To respond to support requests and product inquiries.
  • To maintain platform security and prevent abuse.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who support operations (for example, hosting and analytics) and when required by law.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your Choices

  • You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.
